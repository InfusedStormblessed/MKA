Fatalities List

Here are the button combinations for performing fatalities:

  • F, F, B: Knee to Chest
  • F, F, Y: Heart Rip
  • F, F, X: Head Crush
  • D, D, B: Kick
  • D, D, A: Arm Break
  • D, D, Y: Rib Rip
  • D, D, X: Spine Rip
  • U, U, B: Gut Punch
  • U, U, A: Hit With Arm
  • U, U, Y: Arm Rip
  • U, U, X: Head Rip
  • F, B, B: Right Hook
  • F, B, Y: Femur Rip
  • F, B, X: Rip in Half
  • B, F, Y: Double Arm Rip

Controller Legend:

B: Back
